## Credit Committee Memo CC-991 - Meridian Industrial risk review

Prepared by: Harbor Trust Credit Risk

Subject account: Meridian Industrial Corp (ACC-8847)

Committee date: 2025-10-24

Review period: Q3 2025

## Recommendation

Approve the third amendment (CFA-2025-03) with heightened monitoring. Meridian's working-capital cycle lengthened to 74 days from 58 days year-over-year. Supplier past-due notices (INV-4401, INV-4412) corroborate stretch in payables.

## Key risks

1. Covenant headroom under 0.35x after the temporary leverage relief.
2. Concentration: top three customers represent 41% of Meridian receivables.
3. Management guidance implies Q4 free cash flow near break-even if collections do not improve by mid-November 2025.

## Monitoring plan

Weekly cash report from Meridian treasury; monthly covenant certificate; escalate to special assets if leverage prints above 3.60x on any interim certificate.
