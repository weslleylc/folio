## Meridian Industrial Q3 2025 Earnings Call - Excerpt

Company: Meridian Industrial Corp (ticker: MRDI) - ACC-8847

Call date: 2025-11-05

## CFO commentary

CFO Dana Okonkwo: Working capital was a headwind this quarter. Days sales outstanding rose as two large OEM customers pushed payment timing. We are actively remediating collections and have amended our revolving credit facility with Harbor Trust to provide temporary leverage covenant headroom through March. We expect cash conversion to improve in Q4 if those receivables clear as planned.

## Analyst Q&amp;A

Analyst: How close are you to the leverage covenant after the amendment? CFO: We printed roughly 3.41x at quarter-end against the amended 3.75x cap. We have limited headroom and are managing spend carefully. Reference ACC-8847 in any follow-up with IR.
