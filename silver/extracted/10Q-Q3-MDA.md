## Form 10-Q - Management Discussion (Liquidity) - Meridian Industrial

Registrant: Meridian Industrial Corp (ACC-8847)

Period ended: 2025-09-30

Filing date: 2025-11-07

## Liquidity and Capital Resources

As of September 30, 2025, cash and cash equivalents were USD 41.2 million, compared with USD 63.8 million at December 31, 2024. The decrease primarily reflects higher working capital investment and capital expenditures.

On October 21, 2025, we entered into a third amendment to our revolving credit agreement increasing the maximum consolidated net leverage ratio to 3.75x through March 31, 2026. Management believes existing liquidity, together with the amended facility, is adequate for the next twelve months, though covenant headroom remains limited if receivables continue to age.

Trade payables include amounts past contractual due dates with certain suppliers; see related party and supplier concentration disclosures. Account reference ACC-8847.
